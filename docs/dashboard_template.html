Read this before drawing conclusions. Nothing here is a finding yet. Observations are sampled near new moon so lunar brightening is controlled, and nights with poor coverage are discarded. But thin cloud is not corrected for, and thin cloud dims a city in exactly the way a partial outage does. A single low night is more likely weather than a blackout. Read the ranking, not any individual number.

Known bias. Generators and rooftop solar dim less during an outage and they track wealth, so this systematically under-detects outages in richer areas and richer countries.

What the number means. The share of a city's normally lit area sitting below half its own usual brightness. Because "usual" is set at the 90th percentile of that city's own nights, a typical night sits below it by construction, so the absolute level runs high for everyone. The comparison between cities is the informative part, not the level.
